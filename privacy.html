ONE BLOOD RADIO — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ONE BLOOD RADIO ("the Bot") handles information when you use the Bot on Discord.

1. Information We Collect

ONE BLOOD RADIO is designed to process only information reasonably necessary to operate its Discord features.

Depending on the features being used, the Bot may process:

  • Discord User ID
  • Discord Server (Guild) ID
  • Discord Channel ID
  • Discord Voice Channel ID
  • Discord username or display information provided by Discord
  • Commands and interactions sent to the Bot
  • Technical information required for the Bot to operate

The Bot does not intentionally collect passwords, payment information, private messages, or other sensitive personal information.

2. How Information Is Used

Information processed by ONE BLOOD RADIO may be used to:

  • Execute Discord commands.
  • Identify users who interact with the Bot.
  • Identify servers where the Bot is installed.
  • Operate within authorized voice channels.
  • Provide music and radio playback functionality.
  • Maintain necessary Bot configuration.
  • Troubleshoot technical problems.
  • Improve the reliability and functionality of the Bot.

3. Voice Channel Information

ONE BLOOD RADIO may access voice channel information necessary to join a voice channel and play music or radio audio.

The Bot does not intentionally record or store users' voice conversations. The purpose of the Bot is to provide music and radio playback, not to monitor or record users.

4. Messages and Commands

The Bot may process messages or interactions containing commands addressed to it.

The Bot may need information such as the user's Discord ID, server ID, channel information, and command information to perform the requested action.

The Bot does not intentionally store the contents of users' private conversations.

5. Data Storage

If ONE BLOOD RADIO stores information, it is limited to information reasonably necessary for its operation and configuration.

This may include:

  • Server configuration
  • User identifiers
  • Channel identifiers
  • Bot settings

We do not sell or rent user information to third parties.

6. Third-Party Services

ONE BLOOD RADIO may rely on third-party services, including Discord and external music or radio stream providers, to provide its functionality.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

ONE BLOOD RADIO does not control the privacy practices of third-party services.

7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information processed by the Bot.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Bot's functionality, maintain configuration, troubleshoot technical problems, or comply with applicable requirements.

When information is no longer necessary, it may be deleted or removed.

9. Data Deletion Requests

If you want information associated with your Discord account or server to be deleted from ONE BLOOD RADIO's systems, you may contact the ONE BLOOD development team through the official ONE BLOOD Discord community.

Requests may be subject to reasonable verification to prevent unauthorized deletion.

10. Children's Privacy

ONE BLOOD RADIO does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

Users should comply with Discord's age requirements and applicable laws when using Discord and the Bot.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Bot's features, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

The "Last Updated" date will be updated when significant changes are made.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, data deletion requests, or other concerns, please contact the ONE BLOOD development team through the official ONE BLOOD Discord community.
